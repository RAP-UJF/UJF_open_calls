The fourth THETA 2 competition is open from 27 August to 21 October 2026. It supports energy transformation and modernisation with applicable research results. Maximum whole-project aid intensity is 90% in PP1, 60% in PP2 and 85% in PP3; these are not individual-participant rates.

Reality check: UJF may lead. PP2 requires at least one enterprise that will use the results; PP1 has its own priority objectives and application-guarantor rules.
